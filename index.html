Principles

How the lab is built — and why the agents do not get a blank check.

01

Read-only by default

Agents observe first. Writes are gated, confirmed, and reversible.

02

Sanitize before the model

Raw syslog never meets an LLM. The model sees a problem, not a credential.

03

Fail closed

A 7-layer guardrail. Risk gates of auto / approve / page-out. Unclear policy means nothing fires.

04

Evidence, not screenshots

What leaves the wall is a sealed badge. HMAC-audited runs. Immutable GAIT.

05

Honest coverage

Publish what actually works on Arista, Nokia SR Linux, and FRR — not a marketing matrix.

06

Human-approved change

RFC 6241 confirmed-commit with rollback. Automation proposes; a person owns the blast radius.
